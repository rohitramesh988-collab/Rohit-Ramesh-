# Roadmap: The Whistle

Status: DRAFT · Profile: Balanced · Format: 60 s, 16:9, 1920x1080, 24 fps

## 1. Concept
- **Logline:** A keeper on a lonely rock greets his neighbour on the next rock every dawn in whistled speech, until the morning no whistle comes back.
- **The turn:** Before: a call and a reply, a daily ritual. / After: he answers the silence himself, and the rock gives something back.
- **Ending:** He whistles the neighbour's own reply phrase. After a long beat a faint whistle returns across the water. We cannot tell if it is the neighbour or the cliff's echo. He switches off the lamp as the sun clears the haze.
- **How code shows it:** 3D realistic, three.js with software WebGL (slow but possible here). The keeper is a figure at a distance and in back-lit close-ups of hands, lips and the lamp, never a detailed face. The rocks, sea, lamp and haze are modelled in full.
- **World test:** every image comes from this place: salt-crusted lens, a brass lamp switch, a tally scratched in plaster, a whistle shaped by cupped fingers, a second rock with an unlit tower.

**Tempo:** contemplative, about 7.5 s per shot, because the film is about waiting for a reply. Shots 8. BPM none (sound-led, no beat grid).

## 3. Look
- **Base preset:** cinematic realistic, custom Salt Dawn.
- **Changes:** overcast pre-dawn, pearl grey and rose (#C9B8B2, #8E9AA3, #D9A38F), lamp amber #E8A33D, telephoto compression, shallow focus. Finish passes chosen at the style frame.
- **References used:** none (stills search blocked in this session).

## 4. Sound identity
- **Chosen concept:** pressure, with one clear whistle breaking through.
- **Palette:** synthesised wind and surf, soft low-passed room tone, the whistle as a synthesised voice.
- **Signature sound:** the whistle phrase, returning changed in S07 and S08.
- **Silence:** S05, about 5 s of surf only.
- **Voice · music:** no narrator (on-screen text instead) · music written in code: sparse drone and a four-note figure from the whistle phrase.
- **Ambience per space · shared reverb:** one bed each for the gallery, the interior and the open sea; one open-air reverb.

## 5. Hook
Frame 1: the lamp is already dying and the hand pulls the switch; a whistle starts on the same frame.

## 6. Risks
| Risk | Why | Plan |
|---|---|---|

## 7. Director card
- **Structure:** call, reply, repeat, then the reply is missing and he supplies it.
- **Look:** Salt Dawn (user) · **Camera:** patient telephoto, one repeated locked frame (S02 and S05) · **Motion:** settle
- **Tempo:** contemplative · **Transitions:** plain cuts, one match cut S04 to S05 · **Sound:** pressure plus one clear whistle
- **Light:** soft pre-dawn that warms by the end; the lamp is the only warm point · **Signature:** the whistle phrase, returning changed
