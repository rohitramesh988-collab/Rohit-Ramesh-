# Script: The Whistle

Status: DRAFT · Version: 1 · Runtime: 60 s · Voice: none (on-screen text and whistles)

## Angle
- **Logline:** A keeper greets his neighbour on the next rock every dawn in whistled speech, until the morning no whistle comes back.
- **Why this angle:** The lighthouse stays quiet; the people speak without words across the water, so the story is carried by sound and absence.
- **The turn:** Before: a call and a reply, a daily ritual. / After: he answers the silence himself, and the rock gives something back.
- **Ending:** A faint whistle returns, echo or friend, and he switches the lamp off at sunrise. The film never says which.
