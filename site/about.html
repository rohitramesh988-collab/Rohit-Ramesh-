About Me

An independent consultant, not an agency

Rohit Ramesh, freelance digital marketing consultant with 8 years of experience, helping small businesses, startups and growing companies build a stronger online presence through practical, honest digital marketing.

Digital marketing should solve real problems, not just improve numbers

My role isn't to sell SEO, a website, or a set of social posts. It's to understand what's actually holding a business back from getting more of the right enquiries, and to recommend only what genuinely addresses that. Some businesses need better search visibility. Others need a functioning website before anything else matters. A few need nothing more than a clearer set of Google Business listings.

Every business is different, so there's no template strategy applied across clients. Before recommending anything, I try to understand the business, the industry, the customers, the goals, the constraints and what's realistic given the budget and timeline involved.

How I work

The starting point is always how people search today, on Google, on Google Maps, and increasingly by asking AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude directly. A plan gets built around that reality rather than around what worked five years ago.

Scope, timeline and expected outcome are agreed in writing before any work starts, so there's no ambiguity about what you're paying for or when you'll see it. I take on only a few clients at a time, which means your business gets proper attention rather than a slot in a shared queue. There are no account managers and no handoffs. You work directly with me, and I stay accountable for the result.

What I won't do

  • Guarantee rankings or results, since no honest consultant can promise an outcome that search engines control.
  • Recommend a service because it's popular, rather than because it solves your specific problem.
  • Take on more clients than I can give proper attention to.
  • Use fear-based pitches or inflate what digital marketing alone can fix.

Who this is for

Businesses I work best with

Mostly small businesses, startups and growing companies who are experts in their own field but want straightforward guidance on marketing, not more jargon.

Small & local businesses

Businesses that depend on being found nearby, on Google Maps, on local search and increasingly on AI search tools.

Startups & founders

Early-stage teams that need a website and search presence built properly from the start, without unnecessary complexity or cost.

Professional & B2B services

Consultants, firms and service businesses where trust and clear positioning matter as much as visibility.

Frequently asked

Questions people ask before getting started

Are you an agency or a freelancer?

An independent freelancer. Digital Growth Solutions is my own practice, not an agency with multiple staff. That's a deliberate choice, it means direct, personalised attention rather than being passed between account managers.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Most current work is with Indian businesses, but the services themselves, SEO, AI search visibility, websites and social media, apply anywhere. If you're outside India, mention it when you get in touch and we can work out whether it's a good fit.

How many clients do you take on at once?

Deliberately few. The pricing and process are built around giving each client's business real attention rather than spreading thin across as many accounts as possible.

Can I see your qualifications or certifications?

I'd rather you judge the work on the thinking behind it and on a direct conversation about your business than on a list of certificates. Happy to talk through my approach and experience on a call.

Have a specific situation in mind?

The AI guide can point you to the right service and pricing in a minute. For anything that needs judgement, a quote or a conversation, reach out directly.
