Services & Pricing

A la carte services, priced clearly

Take one service or combine a few. Everything, scope, timeline and expected result, is agreed in writing before any work starts. Prices below are per service; domain and hosting subscriptions are billed directly to you where relevant.

Core offerings

The five services most businesses start with

Local & AI Search Visibility

₹7,500

Get found on Google Maps and on AI search engines such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude. This covers setting up and optimising your business listings and structured business information so both traditional search engines and AI tools can find, understand and recommend you when someone nearby is looking for what you offer.

SEO

₹4,500/month

Basic SEO to improve your website's Google ranking. Using the exact words your customers actually search for, so you're found the moment they're looking, rather than optimising for terms nobody searches.

Website Development

₹6,000

Setup, domain and hosting configuration, and plugin installation on WordPress, so Google can find and understand your site easily and it works properly on any screen. No backups included. Domain and hosting subscription costs are borne by the client; this fee covers setup and ongoing management only.

Social Media Marketing

₹4,000/platform

Organic posts and graphics on Facebook, Instagram or LinkedIn, priced per platform. Regular photos and updates about your business, shared consistently, so followers stay aware of what you offer.

Videos That Show Your Work

₹3,000/video

Short videos that show your work clearly and build customer trust, so people feel confident choosing you before they've even spoken to you.

Frequently asked

Pricing and scope questions

Do domain and hosting costs come out of the website development fee?

No. Domain and hosting subscription costs are billed directly to you by the provider and are borne by the client. The ₹6,000 website development fee covers setup and ongoing management only.

Is social media marketing pricing per platform or for all platforms together?

Per platform. ₹4,000 covers organic posts and graphics on one platform, such as Facebook, Instagram or LinkedIn.

Can I combine multiple services?

Yes. Most clients combine a few core services, for example SEO with Local & AI Search Visibility, or a new website with ongoing SEO. Pricing is added up per service, and everything is agreed in writing before work starts.

Do you offer ongoing monthly support instead of one-off projects?

Yes, through an Annual Maintenance Contract, priced monthly based on the number of services you choose to have maintained. Pricing depends on your specific mix, so it's discussed directly.

Are graphic design and content marketing available on their own?

Yes. Both are available on request and can be added alongside any core service or taken on their own.
